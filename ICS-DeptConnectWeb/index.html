HISTORY

The Genesis of Innovation

The Institute for Computer Studies (ICS) was founded to address the growing need for highly skilled IT professionals in Northern Mindanao. Established in 20XX, the department quickly grew, adopting an innovative curriculum that combines theoretical knowledge with rigorous practical application. Our foundation is built on the pillars of academic excellence, ethical computing, and community engagement.

A Global Perspective in Tech

Our vision is to become the leading center for computing education and research in the region, recognized globally for producing innovative, ethical, and industry-ready graduates. We aim to drive technological advancement through strategic partnerships, continuous curriculum modernization, and fostering a culture of lifelong learning among our faculty and students.
