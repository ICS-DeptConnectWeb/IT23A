BE ONE OF US

Step into a future powered by innovation and technology!

The Institute for Computer Studies welcomes all aspiring tech enthusiasts to join our growing community of learners, creators, and innovators. Whether you dream of becoming a software engineer, a cybersecurity expert, or a game developer — your journey begins here.

What We Offer:

  • Hands-on Learning: Practical projects and real-world applications of technology.
  • Expert Faculty: Learn from experienced IT professionals and educators.
  • Modern Facilities: Updated computer laboratories and high-speed internet access.
  • Industry Connections: Internship and employment opportunities with partner companies.
  • Active Student Organizations: Join clubs and activities that promote teamwork and creativity.
  • Career-Ready Curriculum: Courses designed for today’s competitive tech industry.

Student Life and Activities

Our students don’t just learn — they experience technology in action! From coding competitions and hackathons to tech expos and community outreach, there’s always something exciting happening in the department.

Admission Details:

Interested in joining us? Here’s how to apply:

  1. Visit the Registrar’s Office or official school website.
  2. Fill out the Application Form and choose your desired program.
  3. Submit the required documents (Form 138, good moral certificate, birth certificate, etc.).
  4. Wait for your entrance exam/interview schedule.
  5. Get ready to become part of our tech community!
